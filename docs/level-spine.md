# Level spine (scaffolding freeze)

**This document owns** how Open Cutaway is numbered and taught from first block lesson to industrial sites: what a level is, the town, the three rungs, need order, verbs, collectibles, bans, and climate. It only changes when the owner reopens a closed question.
**It does not own** object-card copy, art files, JSON Schema, or shipped screens. Do not build Learn levels until this file is the brief.

Closed from the 2026-08-18–19 owner questionnaire. Unbuilt. Not a lesson catalog.

## What a level is

A level is **several objects from different facets of the industrial landscape**, unified by **one through-line**: a need the child already has (lights on, trash gone, a toy in the shop). Other systems stay visible on the busy block but are not the test until their lesson.

Growth is **widen, then deepen**: first, many needs on the same regular-world picture; later, follow a need off the sidewalk.

## The town

One **stylized block**, denser than real life (occupational density: jobs, vehicles, shops, civic work in one view). Not a map of a real town. Not a mascot town. People are **jobs**, not named characters.

The whole block is drawn from the start. Off-need objects stay **visually quiet** until their through-line (still there, not the test). Quiet/dim must not rely on color alone.

**Canon facts about this town**

| Fact | Closed as |
| --- | --- |
| Power | Hydro. Lights (and electric heat/cool) trace to the dam. The dam may sit outside town (upriver). |
| Heat/cool | Electric. Shares the dam campus with lights: **two visits, two through-lines**, same place, because it is true here. |
| Sewage + storm | **Combined sewer**. Inlet and flush share a blended flow to the wastewater plant. Drinking water is a **different** chain. Never imply the tap is sewer. |
| Far sites | May sit **outside town** (dam, landfill, factory, terminal). “Other side of town” was a default, not a law. |
| Goods | The shop/food through-line includes the toy. Medium: truck then rail. Far: factory the train goes to. Crossing still teaches tracks as street furniture; rail is quiet until goods. |
| Shared campuses | Only when the real system shares them (combined plant; dam for lights and heat). Do not merge sites just to save levels. |

Industry also appears as a **quiet hint** on the block (stack, crane, towers, river), then as a **real new scene**. After each need’s trip, **return to the block**. Do not hop plant-to-plant without going home.

## Three rungs (every through-line)

| Rung | Geography | Player verbs (the test) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Nearest the child** — inlet by the curb, mailbox by a house, pole on the street | Name (real word + short gloss), function, find it in the busy picture |
| 2 | **The carrier** — pipes under the street, the truck, the wires, the rail leaving the shop | Order the three rungs, read the cutaway |
| 3 | **The facility** — plant, dam, yard, factory, terminal (in-game only) | This path not that one, match a job. Climate/pollution is taught here: why the dam; why we clean the blend before the river |

Jobs may be visible on rungs 1–2; they are not the test until rung 3. A few **job hats** can still drop on the block (carrier, trash crew) as collectibles, not as the Learn test.

**Challenge** (optional, after a need’s three rungs): recall and path-choice, no new objects. Does not unlock the next need. Cosmetic stars only.

## Numbered shape (~31 sittings, not 20)

Rung 1 is **eleven** block lessons because combined sewer is taught as **rain, then flush** (the join is the point). Fuel and comms stay separate.

Then each of **ten deepen through-lines** gets **two beats** (rung 2, then rung 3). Combined sewer’s two widen lessons share **one** deepen chain to the plant (two campus visits if storm-through-line and sewage-through-line are tested separately at the same plant).

| Phase | Count | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Widen (rung 1) | 11 | Same busy block, one through-line per sitting |
| Deepen (rungs 2–3) | 20 | Per need: carrier, then facility; then home |
| Optional Challenge | up to 10 | After that need’s three rungs; not a gate |
| Synthesis | not frozen | No extra “boss” level required in this pass |

A sitting is **~10–15 minutes**. Labels are short. Adult reads the longer plant/dam text. Ages 7–12 with co-play; younger children need the adult.

## Widen order (rung 1)

| # | Through-line | On the block (examples, unfrozen nouns) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Get across | Signal, crosswalk, rail crossing as street furniture |
| 2 | Lights | Pole, wire, transformer |
| 3 | Drink | Tap / meter / hydrant — the **clean** chain |
| 4 | Trash | Can, alley, truck |
| 5 | Rain | Puddle, inlet — street water that is **not** drinking water |
| 6 | Flush | Lateral / cleanout — joins the rain in the combined pipe |
| 7 | Heat/cool | Heat pump / outdoor kit (electric) |
| 8 | Mail | Mailbox, carrier |
| 9 | Goods | Shop, toy on a shelf |
| 10 | Fuel | Station as a street object |
| 11 | Comms | Cabinet, pole gear |

Deepen order follows the same through-lines (lights’ dam before heat’s second dam visit). Exact rung-2/rung-3 nouns stay unfrozen; the **shape** is frozen.

## Collectibles (never a gate)

Progress = which rungs of which through-lines are finished. **No** score, timer, or high-score test.

One album, **three shelves**:

| Shelf | What |
| --- | --- |
| Objects | Cards for finds on the busy block |
| Jobs | Hats (block crews and plant jobs) |
| Places | Campus badges (dam, combined-sewer plant, rail-yard/factory, …) |

Need badges are **tab headers**, not a fourth collection. Challenge stars sit on the card, cosmetic.

## Art

Original drawings for the busy block, cutaways, and jobs. CC0 / US public-domain photos for far sites, with a row in `docs/ATTRIBUTION.md`. No identifiable people. No unlicensed scrapes. Do not copy inspiration books.

## IRL and safety

Plants, dams, yards, tracks, cabinets, factories: **in-game only**. IRL looking is sidewalk objects, never a quest, never a gate. No life list in this pass. Object cards keep `safety.approachLiveGear: never`.

## Banned

Tycoon / city-builder. Mascot town or cute infrastructure animals. Mapping a real hometown or a real family’s house. Hats/cards/badges opening the next rung. Quests to visit industrial sites. Implying tap water is sewer water. Timers or high scores as the test. Scavenger hunts that unlock lessons.

Climate is **taught** at rung 3 (why the dam; why treat water), not a stealth sermon and not a level-1 lecture.

## Do not build yet

No hydrant lesson, no plant scene, no album UI, until object cards and schemas follow this spine. `docs/candidates.md` remains unfrozen **nouns**; this file owns **order and shape**.
