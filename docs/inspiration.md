# Inspiration (do not copy)

Open Cutaway is inspired by two books about reading the built world. Inspiration is not a license to copy.

## The books

- David Macaulay, *Underground* — a cutaway street versus stacked systems in the soil.
- Brian Hayes, *Infrastructure: A Guide to the Industrial Landscape* — the everyday industrial landscape is readable and significant.

## Do not copy

Do not copy text, photos, drawings, chapter lists, or taxonomy from either book. Do not scrape them. Do not add their files to this repository. Do not paraphrase a chapter into a “lesson.”

Write original kid-facing copy. Draw original cutaways, object portraits, and chain-strips. For photographs in v0, use only US public domain or CC0 sources with a row in `docs/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Visual language (later content)

Learn-mode graphics should mix original cutaways/cross-sections, object portraits, and chain-strips. This is not a node-link graph explorer. No cute anthropomorphic infrastructure.
